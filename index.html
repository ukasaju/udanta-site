Udanta

उदन्त — Nepali news, gathered

Udanta brings headlines from Nepali news publishers into one feed. Stories covered by several outlets are grouped together, so you can see how a story is being reported rather than reading one version of it.

  • Choose नेपाली for Nepali-language papers, or English for English-language ones — the two are never mixed.
  • Gold and silver rates, exchange rates, and the Kalimati produce board.
  • Local weather and a Bikram Sambat calendar.

Headlines and summaries belong to their publishers. Tapping a story opens it at the source, so the publisher receives the visit.
